What is Maho?

U&I is a volunteer-driven NGO transforming lives through education. We work with children from underserved communities, providing after-school learning support, life skills training, and career guidance to help them break the cycle of poverty.

Maho is a community-driven center under U&I, dedicated to empowering underserved children through education and mentorship. We spend 3 hours a week providing quality after-school care, holistic development opportunities, and a nurturing environment to help the children realize their full potential.

Restoring trust and ambition to boys growing up in residential care

Boys in shelter care need more than tuition. They need steady male role models, space to talk about how they feel, friendships they can rely on, and a path to work. Vetted volunteers commit to weekly sessions inside the shelter, turning isolation into belonging.

Holistic boy-child development

Three areas of work that help boys heal, learn and prepare for independent life.

Academic foundation and STEM

Closing literacy and numeracy gaps with hands-on science, circuit kits, computer basics and personal study plans.

Emotional resilience and confidence

Safe, judgment-free sessions where boys learn to put feelings into words and support each other.

Life skills and vocational readiness

Football, spoken English, budgeting and trade apprenticeships for older boys preparing to leave the shelter.
