# December fundraising plan: ₹3,00,000 in 31 days

Target: ₹3,00,000 (Dec 1 to Dec 31). That is about ₹9,700 per day, or ₹70,000 per week.

## The honest truth first
- **The website does not raise money by itself.** It collects it. In small NGO campaigns, 70 to 80% of donations come from people the team *personally asks* (family, friends, colleagues, college network, volunteers' families, local businesses). Plan for the website to be the place people pay, and for **people-to-people outreach** to bring them there.
- Nobody gives to a page they land on cold. Give them a reason, a deadline and a person who asked.

## 3. Who to ask (build a list of 100+ names this week)
Put in a private spreadsheet (never in the repo): name, relationship, who asks them, amount asked, status (asked / pledged / paid / thanked).
- **Tier A (individuals ₹12,000+):** family, senior contacts, professors, alumni, your parents' networks, colleagues with spare income. Ask personally by call or in person.
- **Tier B (₹1,500 to ₹5,000):** friends, volunteers, their families, classmates, workplace groups.
- **Tier C (small, ₹100 to ₹500):** broad social sharing, college clubs, WhatsApp groups.
- **Organisations:** local businesses, companies' CSR/HR teams, colleges, RWAs, clubs (Rotary/Lions), year-end employee giving. Send a one-page PDF with the ask and the 80G detail.
- **Matching gifts:** ask 2 or 3 supporters to match donations up to a set amount for a week ("Every rupee doubled until Dec 10"). This is the single most effective tactic.

## 4. Messages (templates; personalise with a real sentence about the person)
**Personal ask (WhatsApp/DM):**
> Hi [Name], I'm volunteering with U&I x Maho, supporting boys in a shelter home in Bengaluru with mentorship and education. In December we're trying to raise ₹3,00,000 to fund [what it pays for]. Would you consider sponsoring [₹X, which covers Y]? It takes two minutes at [link], and donations are eligible for 80G [if confirmed]. Happy to tell you more about what we do. Thank you!

**Group/social post:** a real story (with consent, no child's face or full name) + the goal + a live progress number + the link + the deadline.

**Corporate email:** short intro, what we do, the specific programme asked (₹50,000 life-skills programme), the 80G/12A details, a contact person, and an offer to share a report.

**Weekly update (post every Sunday):** ₹ raised so far, number of donors, one short story, thank-you shout-outs (with permission), what's left.

## 6. Donor care (this is how you get repeat gifts)
- Thank within 24 hours, by name where possible; receipt within 48 hours.
- After Dec 31, send an impact report within 2 weeks ("your ₹1,500 bought…").
- Offer a monthly giving option after the campaign for people who want to continue.

## 7. Rules we keep
- **Never exaggerate or invent** numbers, stories or impact. Donors and payment providers check.
- **Child protection:** no faces, no full names, no identifying details; only consented photos.
- Never promise tax benefits we haven't confirmed in writing with U&I.
- No pressure tactics, no fake urgency. A true deadline (Dec 31) is enough.
- Keep donor personal details private. They live only in the payment provider and the NGO's records.
